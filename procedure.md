### **For Continuous Datatype :**

1. Provide a value for each attribute and then click the **ADD** button to input these values into the table.
2. Repeat step 1 until the trained dataset contains a total of 6 rows.
3. Now, enter a value for each attribute to test the algorithm, and then click the **ADD** button.
4. Now, click on the **NEXT** button to initiate the computation phase for the Naive Bayes model.
5. Enter the occurrences of 'MALE' and 'FEMALE' from the DATASET to calculate Prior Probabilities, then click **SUBMIT**.
6. Now, click on **NEXT** button to calculate Mean and Variance.
7. Click on the **MEAN** and then the **VARIANCE** button to see the calculated values.
8. Now, click on the **NEXT** button to calculate Likelihood Probabilities for each fields.
9. Input the required data, starting with 'MALE' and then clicking the **SUBMIT** button, and subsequently for 'FEMALE'. Finally, click **NEXT** to proceed to the next set.
10. Now, calculate the Posterior Probabilities by entering the required fields for P(MALE | NEW INSTANCE) and click on the **SUBMIT** button.
    <ul type="circle">
    <li>Click on the 'Prior Probability' tab given on the left side of the page to view the required values.</li>
    <li>Next, click on the 'Likelihood Probability' tab and and observe the necessary values.</li>
    </ul>

11. Repeat the previous step(10) to calculate the Posterior Probabilities for P(FEMALE | NEW INSTANCE).
12. After submitting the values, click on the **NEXT** button to access the final result.
 
### **For Categorical Datatype :**

1. Provide a value for each attribute and then click the **ADD** button to input these values into the table.
2. Repeat step 1 until the trained dataset contains a total of 6 rows.
3. Now, enter a value for each attribute to test the algorithm, and then click the **ADD** button.
4. Now, click on the **NEXT** button to initiate the computation phase for the Naive Bayes model.
5. Enter the occurrences of 'YES' and 'NO' from the DATASET to calculate Prior Probabilities, then click **SUBMIT**.
6. Now, click on the **NEXT** button to calculate Likelihood Probability.
7. Enter the occurrences of each attribute based on their classes in the 'YES' column of the **CALCULATION** table, and then click the **SUBMIT** button.
8. Repeat step 7 for the 'NO' column and click on the **NEXT** button.
9. If, zero values are present for any likelihood probability, the **Laplace Smoothing** section will appear.
10. Otherwise, calculate the Posterior Probabilities by entering the required fields for P(YES|NEW INSTANCE) and then click on the **SUBMIT** button.
    <ul type="circle">
    <li>Click on the 'Prior Probability' tab given on the left side of the page to view the required values.</li>
    <li>Next, click on the 'Likelihood Probability' tab and and observe the necessary values.</li>
    </ul>

11. Repeat the above step to find P(NO|NEW INSTANCE).
12. After submitting their values, click on **NEXT** button to know the final result.
